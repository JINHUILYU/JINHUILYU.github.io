This paper proposes DSVA, an iterative refinement multi-agent framework for translating natural language (NL) requirements into Metric Temporal Logic (MTL). The framework leverages retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) and large language models (LLMs) within a multi-agent system to achieve high-fidelity formal specification, addressing key challenges in requirements engineering for real-time systems.
